# Brief — <project>

> **What is true about this project no matter which screen you are on.** The things an agent would
> otherwise ask again at the start of every session: what this is, who opens it, how it speaks.
>
> `UI-STACK.md` is the technical contract, written by a developer at kickoff. This is the product
> contract, and it belongs to the designers.
>
> **Nobody is expected to sit down and fill this in.** It starts almost empty and fills itself:
> when a session needs something that is not here yet, it gets asked once and written down here, so
> it is never asked again. Filling a section in advance is welcome and saves a question — leaving
> it blank costs one question, once.
>
> A blank section means *nobody has decided yet*. Write `n/a` when the answer is genuinely "this
> does not apply", so the two can be told apart.

## Visual direction

*Where the look comes from: a Figma file, a brand book, a site to sit next to, or "decided in the

## Constraints

*Anything true of every screen. Languages. Whether it has to work on a phone. Accessibility beyond
the floor the audit already enforces. Whether it will be printed or shown on a shared screen.
Anything the client has stated outright.*

## What this is not

*Explicitly out of scope, and — more useful — things that were considered and rejected. A rejected
idea that is not written down comes back every two months.*

## Open questions
